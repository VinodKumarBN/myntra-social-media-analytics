# myntra-social-media-analytics# Myntra Social Media Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

The Myntra Social Media Analytics project analyzes social media performance across multiple platforms using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to understand audience engagement, follower growth, hashtag performance, content effectiveness, and marketing campaign performance to support data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze engagement across Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube.
- Compare follower growth across social media platforms.
- Evaluate hashtag performance using average clicks per post.
- Compare the performance of videos, images, and carousels.
- Analyze campaign engagement and budget efficiency.
- Visualize weekly follower growth and identify peak growth periods.
- Explore the relationship between advertising spend and follower growth.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis and visualization.
- **PivotTables** — Summarizing and comparing metrics.
- **Excel Charts** — Visualizing engagement, campaign performance, and follower growth.
- **Excel Formulas** — Calculating engagement rates, growth metrics, averages, and campaign efficiency.
- **Power Query** — Splitting hashtags into individual rows for hashtag-level analysis.
- **AI Tools** — Assisting with formula development, troubleshooting, and analysis ideas.

## 📈 Project Analysis

### 1. Platform Analysis
- Compared engagement rates across platforms.
- Examined follower growth rates.
- Visualized engagement rate against advertising spend.
- Developed recommendations for platform strategy.

### 2. Hashtag & Content Strategy
- Identified individual hashtags from the dataset.
- Compared post counts and average clicks by hashtag.
- Compared content types, including videos, images, and carousels.
- Explored content strategy opportunities.

### 3. Campaign Effectiveness
- Calculated total and average impressions, likes, and clicks.
- Compared campaign engagement with campaign budgets.
- Evaluated engagement efficiency using engagement per unit of budget.

### 4. Follower Retention & Loyalty
- Visualized weekly follower growth across platforms.
- Identified the week with the highest follower gain.
- Used a moving average to smooth weekly growth trends.
- Created a scatter plot to examine advertising spend against net follower change.

## 📊 Key Deliverables

- Excel workbook containing the analysis.
- PivotTables summarizing social media performance.
- Charts comparing platforms, content types, campaigns, and follower growth.
- Hashtag-level performance analysis.
- Data-driven marketing recommendations.

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how social media data can be transformed into actionable insights. The analysis can help marketing teams understand audience engagement, evaluate content performance, compare campaign efficiency, and make more informed decisions about advertising and platform strategy.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Findings depend on the quality and coverage of the available dataset.
- Engagement per budget is an efficiency metric, not financial ROI, because campaign revenue is not included.
- Results describe the supplied data and should not automatically be interpreted as current Myntra business performance.
